Second Shift Manager

Delete your account

Second Shift Manager is made by Random Idea Studio s.r.o. The app works without an account; you have one only if you signed in with Google or Apple to share a family space, use AI through our server, keep a cloud backup or buy a plan. This page explains how to delete that account and what happens to its data.

In the app

  1. Open Second Shift Manager and go to Settings → Members.
  2. On the card of your account, open the menu ⋮ and choose Delete account.
  3. Confirm. The account is deleted on our server at once.

Without the app

If you no longer have the app, write to randomideastudio.dev@gmail.com from any address and tell us the e-mail address of the Google or Apple account you signed in with. We may ask you to confirm that the account is yours, and we delete it within 30 days. A parent can ask us in the same way to delete the account of their child.

What is deleted

  • Your account with its e-mail address and name, your sessions, memberships, join requests and push tokens.
  • Your cloud backup, your private pages of Mine, your AI usage counts and your donations; the record our purchase provider RevenueCat keeps about you.
  • Every shared space in which you were the last adult, with all its records and photos.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Records you added to a shared space that other members keep using stay there as the family's records.
  • A subscription that pays for a shared space stays with that space, without your account.
  • Feedback you sent (at most one year) and support campaign counts (at most 24 months) stay without the link to your account.
  • An irreversible fingerprint of your sign-in with that month's AI usage counts, until the end of the calendar month, so that a new account does not renew the free AI quota.
  • Our database backups, until they expire (at most 35 days).

Deleting the account does not delete the data on your phone: uninstall the app to delete it there. Details are in the privacy policy of Second Shift Manager.
