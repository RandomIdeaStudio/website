Privacy policy

Version of 17 November 2026. The same text is shown in the app (Settings → About).

Who we are

Second Shift Manager ("the app") is made by Random Idea Studio s.r.o., company ID (IČO) 30264138, Hlavní 53, 756 63 Krhová, Czech Republic, registered in the Commercial Register kept by the Regional Court in Ostrava, file C 105915. We are the controller of the personal data described here. Contact for anything about privacy: randomideastudio.dev@gmail.com. This policy says what the app and our server do with personal data, why, on what legal basis, who else receives it and for how long it is kept. It applies to the app on Android and iOS and to our server.

The short version

  • What you enter is stored on your phone. Without an account nothing of it leaves the phone.
  • With an account you can share a family space; its records are then synced through our server so that your family sees the same data.
  • Usage statistics and crash reports are sent only if you agree. Neither ever contains the content of your records.
  • AI features send only what you ask them to process, with the records they may refer to, and only when you use them.
  • We have no ads, we do not sell or share personal data for advertising and we do not track you across apps or websites.

What we process, why and on what legal basis

The legal bases are those of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), Article 6(1): (b) performance of the contract with you, that is providing the app and its services as described in the terms of use; (c) a legal obligation; (f) our legitimate interests, which we name each time; and (a) your consent.

On your phone

Everything you enter is stored in the app's private storage on your phone: children and other people (names, and optional profile details such as phone numbers, e-mail and postal addresses, birth dates, clothing and shoe sizes, notes, contact people and photos), events (with their place, people and things to bring), tasks (with their time, steps and ticks), household work you record, shopping lists, notes, recipes, favourite activities, school timetables (lessons, subjects, short notes and term dates), wish lists (with prices, notes, shop links and which adult will buy a wish), children's chores and stars, things and places (with expiry dates), cards and documents (Premium: a name, a note and photos of both sides, for example of a loyalty card, an insurance card or an identity document), warranties of purchased things (a name, the day of the purchase, the warranty in months, a note and, with Premium, a photo of the receipt), your private pages of Mine (blocks with a title, text and, if you add them, a date, a time and a tick), expenses, repeating expenses with their payment reminders, budgets, and a log of the last 200 changes of these records (the record's name, what happened, when and who did it). The app also keeps settings, a technical log of the last 200 events on this phone (codes and numbers only, never content), a trash of what was deleted on this phone and automatic backups.

Reminders, the home screen widget, public holidays and the PDFs of a person's week and school timetable are made on the phone from this data; nothing is sent for them. Android's system backup is turned off and the app's data is excluded from iPhone backups.

Automatic backups (about once a week and before risky steps such as leaving a shared space; the last three of each kind) are encrypted with a random key kept in the phone's secure storage and never leave the phone. A manual backup is a file encrypted with your password, saved or shared where you choose. "Export data as JSON" saves the records you can see as one readable, unencrypted file wherever you choose.

"Load from phone contacts" opens the phone's own contact picker; the app has no access to your address book and receives only the one contact you pick, which it fills into the form like a typed entry. Other calendars shown in the app use the phone's calendars only when you turn them on (see below).

Legal basis: performance of the contract (b).

Account and sign-in

You need an account only for sharing, AI through our server, the cloud backup and the paid plans. You sign in with Google or Apple. Our server then receives and keeps the provider, its stable user ID, and the e-mail address and name of that account as the provider shares them (Apple may give a private relay address and gives the name only at the first sign-in), and gives you an account ID of ours. Each sign-in updates them. The e-mail and name are shown to the administrator of a shared space you ask to join, so that they recognise who is asking, and, once you are a member, to the members of the shared space you belong to, next to the family's person your account is paired with (Settings → Members), so that everyone sees which account is whose; they are never shown to anyone else. We can look up an account by its sign-in e-mail, only to give a plan for free or to help you when you write to us. The e-mail and name are never used to contact you or for anything else. Sign-in sessions last at most 60 days unless renewed.

Legal basis: performance of the contract (b).

Shared space

When you create or join a shared space, its records are synced through our server and stored there: everything listed under "On your phone" except settings, the technical log, the trash and automatic backups. The administrator decides who joins (each join request waits for their approval), who each new member is among the family's people, each member's role and what each member sees and may do; a member receives only what the administrator allows them (an adult everything until the administrator limits it, a child by default no expenses), and only adults' devices receive who buys a wish. Every member sees the log of changes with who changed which record. So that a change can be undone from any phone with full access, the log entry of a member with full access also keeps the records that change wrote as they were before it; children and members whose access is limited never receive that part. A deleted record's earlier data can therefore stay in the log, at most until its entry falls out of the newest 200 changes of the space. Photos (Premium), including the photos of cards and documents, are stored in our file storage and sent only to the members of the space whose role may see them. Your private pages of Mine are stored on our server too, but only your own account ever receives them: other members and the administrator never get them, they are not in the log of changes and never sent to AI. They are not end-to-end encrypted on the server. When you leave the space, are removed from it or delete your account, they are deleted from the server.

Legal basis: performance of the contract (b).

Cloud backup

The cloud backup is the same password-encrypted file as a manual backup, stored on our server, one per account. We cannot read it. It is replaced by the next one and deleted with your account.

Legal basis: performance of the contract (b).

AI notes and the AI chat

When you ask the app to process a note or a photo, or a receipt photo of an expense (Fill in the expense with AI in the expense form), or write to the AI chat, the app sends to our server, and our server to Anthropic (the Claude models):

  • the text of the note, the photo, or the chat conversation; a receipt photo goes as the app stores it, without metadata such as the place or the camera;
  • the records it may refer to, which the app always sends with it: the names and kinds (child, adult, contact in the shared space, other contact) of people; the names of places, expense categories, things, favourite activities and shopping lists (never their items); the title, dates, time, place, repeat, people, the person who takes care of it and the activity of events in the next 30 days; the title, date, time, repeat and people of open tasks; and expenses of the last 30 days (title, date, amount, currency, whether paid, who paid, category, person, activity);
  • today's date and short hints for the app's language, such as the currency and the date format.

Never sent: notes, profile details (phone numbers, addresses, birth dates, sizes, contact people, photos), photos of things, cards and documents, a receipt photo you have not chosen to send, wishes, timetables, chores, work records or any other record. On a child's phone only records they may see are sent. The server checks every field against a fixed list and refuses anything else.

Importing a bank statement with AI (Premium, for whoever may add expenses, a child only with Finance from the administrator; Finance → ⋮ → Import a statement with AI): only when you choose it and confirm, the app sends the statement you picked (a PDF, or a photo without metadata such as the place or the camera) to our server, and our server to Anthropic, together with today's date, your default currency and the names of your expense categories; no other record. The statement can hold your account number, balances and the names of the people and shops you paid or who paid you. Our server does not store or log it; it only counts the AI requests the statement uses. Anthropic reads it as for any AI request (see below). Back on your phone you choose which payments to keep: only those are saved as your expenses and incomes, with the date, a short description, the amount, the currency and a category, and the app removes account numbers, IBANs and card numbers from the descriptions before saving. The statement itself is not saved in the app.

Finance with AI (Premium, for whoever sees Finance, a child only with Finance from the administrator; Finance → Discuss with AI): only when you write in this chat, the app sends your message and the conversation before it to our server, and our server to Anthropic, together with the totals of your Finance: for the last 12 months the income and expenses by month and by expense category (with the categories' names), the repeating expenses and incomes (their name, amount and repeat) and your budgets with what this month has used of them. It never sends a single payment or its description, the people of the family, who paid, notes, photos, account numbers, Cards & documents or private pages. Our server does not store or log the conversation or the totals; it only counts the message against the month's AI quota. The conversation stays on your phone until you clear it. Anthropic reads it as for any AI request (see below). A budget the AI suggests is saved only when you confirm it.

Our server does not store or log the content of AI requests; it only counts uses for the monthly quota and requests for the daily limit. Anthropic processes the request as our processor under its commercial terms, does not use it to train its models, and keeps it only for a short time for abuse monitoring (see "How long we keep data"). The AI's proposals are only suggestions: nothing is saved until you confirm it. The chat conversation stays on your phone until you clear it.

So that deleting an account and signing in again does not renew the free AI quota, one thing outlives a deleted account: an irreversible fingerprint of the sign-in (a keyed hash of the provider and its user ID, from which neither can be read) with the numbers of AI uses of that month and of AI requests of that day, nothing else.

Legal basis: performance of the contract (b); for the fingerprint, our legitimate interest in protecting the free quota from abuse (f).

Push messages

While your phone shares a space, it gets a push token from Google Firebase Cloud Messaging (on an iPhone delivered through Apple's push service), and our server keeps it with your account and space, the platform and whether you want notifications from others. The server sends the other members' phones a silent message when something changed (so they sync) and a notification when a member chooses "Notify in the app". The messages carry no content: only the space ID and, for a notification, the kind of change, the record ID and the ID of the member who made it; each phone writes the text from its own data. Without a shared space no token is made. Notifications from others can be turned off in Settings → Reminders.

Legal basis: performance of the contract (b).

Calendars

Other calendars in the app (Standard or Premium, off by default) are read on the phone only while shown, read-only; the app never writes into the phone's calendars, and they are not stored, sent to our server or shared. An .ics file you import is read on the phone.

The subscription link (Standard or Premium, off by default) makes the events of your shared space (title, note, place, dates, times, repeat, filtered by the people you choose) available at a secret address that calendar apps fetch. Anyone who has the address can read these events, and the calendar services that fetch it keep their own copy. Our server keeps a hash of the secret, the filter, the time zone and the last built calendar text; you can make a new address or turn it off at any time.

Legal basis: performance of the contract (b).

Usage statistics (only with your consent)

If you agree (one question about usage statistics and crash reports appears once on the home screen; you can change each answer any time in Settings → About → Privacy), the app sends Google Firebase Analytics events such as "app opened", "tab opened: calendar", "record created: task, by hand", "AI note used: through the server, 2–3 proposals" or "Premium screen shown", and three properties: the plan, the number of children and the number of adults in your shared space, both in groups (0, 1, 2, 3 or more). Events never carry titles, names, notes, amounts, dates, record IDs or any text. Firebase adds a random ID of the app installation, the device model, the operating system and app version, the language and an approximate country derived from the IP address. The advertising ID is not collected, Google signals and ad personalisation are off, and no user ID is set. Until you agree, nothing is sent and Analytics collection stays off. A child's phone never sends usage statistics.

The same consent covers the counts of support campaigns on our server (see "Support campaigns").

Legal basis: consent (a), which you can withdraw at any time with the same switch.

Crash reports (only with your consent)

If you agree (the same question as for usage statistics; a separate switch in Settings → About → Privacy), when the app runs into an unexpected error, it sends Google Firebase Crashlytics the type of the error (never its message, which could contain your data), the stack trace (where in the code it happened), the app version, the plan, the platform, the device model and operating system version, and a random Crashlytics installation ID. Native crashes are reported by the Crashlytics SDK as Android or iOS records them. Crash reports contain no content of your records and no identifier of you. Until you agree, nothing is sent and Crashlytics collection stays off. A child's phone never sends crash reports.

Legal basis: consent (a), which you can withdraw at any time with the switch in Settings → About → Privacy.

Problem reports and suggestions

Settings → Feedback sends your message, an optional screenshot you pick, the app version, platform, device model, operating system version and app language, and when you are signed in your account ID and shared space ID. The form shows all of it before sending. Please do not put passwords or health details into the message.

Legal basis: our legitimate interest in answering you and fixing problems (f).

Support campaigns

The app has no ads; instead it sometimes asks for support. To fetch the current campaign it sends our server the app language, and your account when you are signed in (so paying spaces are not asked). Whether and when to show it is decided on the phone; the days you used the app and your answers stay on the phone. A child's phone never shows campaigns. Only if you agreed to usage statistics, our server records that a campaign was shown and which answer you chose, with the time, and when you are signed in your account and space ID; a purchase, donation or free trial within 24 hours of such an answer is linked to it, and when such a trial later turns into a paid subscription, that is recorded with the answer too.

Legal basis: consent (a), the same as for usage statistics.

Purchases and donations (when purchases are enabled)

The paid plans and donations are bought through Google Play or the App Store, which handle the payment; we never see your payment details. RevenueCat manages the subscription state with your account ID as its user ID and receives the store's purchase records. Our server keeps the plan of your space, the product, the store, the expiry and for donations the price. When you delete your account, our server deletes your donations and asks RevenueCat to delete its record of you; a subscription that pays for a shared space stays with the space, without your account, as long as the space needs it.

Legal basis: performance of the contract (b).

Server logs and security

Our server logs the method, the path (which can contain the IDs of a space or a record, never the secret of a subscription link), the status and the duration of requests, never their content, tokens or names. Google Cloud's request logs contain IP addresses, the user agent and the requested address, including the secret address of a subscription link. Limits against abuse keep IP addresses in memory for at most a day.

Legal basis: our legitimate interest in the security and operation of the service (f).

Database backups

To recover from failures, our database can be restored to any moment of the last 7 days, and a weekly copy of it is kept for 35 days in our file storage. The users' photos and cloud backups are not in these copies. What you delete disappears from these copies when they expire.

Legal basis: our legitimate interest in the availability and integrity of the service (f).

Sensitive information

The app does not ask for health data or other special categories of personal data, but its free texts (notes, profile notes, the notes of events and tasks) and the photos of cards and documents can contain them, for example an allergy, a medication or an insurance number, if you choose to enter them. We process such data only to store it, sync it to the members of your space and back it up as you instruct; we never analyse it, never send notes or cards to AI or statistics and never use it for any other purpose. Please enter only what your family needs, and keep identity documents in the app only if you are comfortable with it.

Children

The app is made for parents and other adults who organise a family. Much of the data concerns children, and it is entered by those adults; it is processed to provide the service the adults use (contract, b).

The person who creates a shared space must be an adult (terms of use). A child can use the app on their own phone only when the administrator of a shared space invites them and approves their join request with the role "Child"; there is no other way to join. The child signs in with their own Google or Apple account like any member. On our server we keep the child's account (as for every account), the membership and the records the child adds. What a child sees and may do is set by the administrator, and our server enforces it.

A child's phone never sends usage statistics or crash reports, never shows support campaigns and is never offered the paid plans, nor AI on the free plan.

The legal basis for a child's account is the contract with the parent or other adult who administers the space and invites the child; the app does not rely on a child's consent. Parents and guardians can exercise the child's rights. The administrator can remove a child from the space at any time; the child's account is deleted in Settings → Sharing, members and permissions → Delete account on the child's phone, or on a parent's request to randomideastudio.dev@gmail.com.

The service is not directed to children under 13 as account holders. A child's account exists only under the supervision of a parent who invited them. If you believe a child uses an account without a parent's knowledge, write to us and we will delete it.

Who receives data

We do not sell personal data and do not share it with anyone for advertising. We use these processors, each bound by a data processing agreement:

  • Google Cloud (Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC): our server (Cloud Run), file storage for photos, cloud backups, feedback screenshots and database copies (Cloud Storage) and the server's secrets (Secret Manager), all in the region europe-west1 (Belgium).
  • Neon Inc. (USA): our PostgreSQL database, stored in the AWS region eu-central-1 (Frankfurt, Germany).
  • Google Firebase (Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC): Analytics (only with consent; data kept 2 months), Crashlytics (only with consent; 90 days) and Cloud Messaging.
  • Anthropic PBC (USA): AI notes, the AI chat, the AI import of a bank statement and Finance with AI.
  • RevenueCat, Inc. (USA): subscriptions, when purchases are enabled.

Independent recipients under their own terms, when you use them: Google and Apple for signing in, for the stores and their payments and for push delivery; the calendar services that subscribe to your link; and whoever you share an exported file, a backup, a message or a subscription link with. Other members of your shared space see the space's records as their role allows. We may disclose data when the law requires it.

Transfers outside the EU

Some processors are in the USA or may access data from there (Google, Neon, Anthropic, RevenueCat). Such transfers rely on the European Commission's adequacy decision for the EU-US Data Privacy Framework where the recipient is certified under it, and otherwise on the Commission's standard contractual clauses with additional safeguards. You can ask us for a copy of the safeguards at randomideastudio.dev@gmail.com.

How long we keep data

  • On your phone: until you delete it or uninstall the app. Nothing is deleted for its age: finished tasks, bought shopping items, expenses, recorded household work and other records of the past are kept indefinitely unless you delete them; older ones are only moved to an archive on the phone, which is read when you look back. Deleted records stay in the phone's trash for 30 days, and the log keeps the last 200 changes. The technical log keeps the last 200 events, and automatic backups the last three of each kind.
  • Shared space on our server: indefinitely, until a member deletes the records or the space ends; nothing is deleted for its age. A deletion is kept as a short mark without content for 90 days so that every phone learns of it. A photo is deleted from the server once no record uses it. When the last adult leaves or deletes their account, the space is deleted with its records and files.
  • Account: until you delete it. Join requests and invitations expire after 7 days. A push token is deleted when you sign out, leave the space or delete your account, or when Google reports it invalid. A subscription link's data is deleted when you turn it off, leave the space or delete your account.
  • AI: the content is not stored by us; Anthropic keeps requests for at most 30 days (longer only if a request is flagged for a breach of its usage policy). The counts of uses are kept per account and month; the fingerprint after an account deletion is deleted after the end of that calendar month.
  • Usage statistics: 2 months in Firebase Analytics. Crash reports: 90 days in Crashlytics.
  • Feedback: at most one year, then deleted with its screenshot.
  • Support campaign records: at most 24 months.
  • Purchases: a subscription's record as long as the shared space it pays for exists (without your account once it is deleted); donations until you delete your account. The stores keep their own records of your payments as their terms say.
  • Server logs: 30 days. Database copies: up to 7 days in the restore window and 35 days in the weekly copies.

Deleting your account (Settings → Members → the account's ⋮ menu → Delete account, or on request to randomideastudio.dev@gmail.com) deletes at once your account with its e-mail and name, sessions, memberships, join requests, push tokens, AI counts and your cloud backup, and the shared spaces where you were the last adult with their records and files. The records you added to a space that continues stay there as the family's records. Your donations and RevenueCat's record of you are deleted too. Feedback and campaign records stay without the link to your account, and a subscription stays with the space it pays for; the AI fingerprint stays until the end of the month; the database copies expire as above. Uninstalling the app deletes the data on the phone.

Your rights

You have the right to access your data, to have it corrected, to have it erased, to restrict its processing, to data portability, to object to processing based on legitimate interests, and to withdraw consent at any time without affecting what was done before. In the app you can see and correct everything you entered, export it ("Export data as JSON" in Settings → Backup and restore), turn statistics and crash reports off (Settings → About → Privacy) and delete your account. For anything else, including a copy of the data our server holds about your account, write to randomideastudio.dev@gmail.com; we answer within one month. Parents and guardians can exercise these rights for their children.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority: in the Czech Republic the Office for Personal Data Protection (Úřad pro ochranu osobních údajů, Pplk. Sochora 27, 170 00 Praha 7, www.uoou.gov.cz), or the authority of the country where you live or work.

We make no decisions based solely on automated processing that have legal or similarly significant effects on you. AI proposals are only suggestions for you to confirm.

Users in California and elsewhere

California (CCPA/CPRA). We do not sell personal information and do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising, and we have no actual knowledge of selling or sharing data of consumers under 16. The categories we collect are described above (identifiers such as the account ID and installation IDs; contact details of the sign-in account; the content you enter; usage data with your consent; diagnostic data), for the purposes described above. California residents may request to know, correct and delete their personal information and will not be discriminated against for exercising these rights; write to randomideastudio.dev@gmail.com. We do not use sensitive personal information to infer characteristics.

Other places. The app is available worldwide and processes data as described here wherever you live; where the law of your country gives you further rights, you can exercise them by writing to us.

Security

Data travels encrypted (HTTPS/TLS). On our server and in our file storage it is encrypted at rest by the providers; access to production is limited to the studio's administrator. Backups you make are encrypted with your password, automatic backups with a key in the phone's secure storage. There is no end-to-end encryption of the shared space: our server can technically read its records, but we access them only when needed to run the service or when the law requires it.

Changes

We will update this policy when the app or our processing changes and tell you in the app before an important change takes effect. The date of the version is at the top.
